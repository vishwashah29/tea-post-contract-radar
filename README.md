# Contract Radar for Tea Post

A concept prototype for Sirion's contract-lifecycle brief: a contract record that pushes. It covers what happens after signing: a searchable record of signed contracts, and an owner and renewal radar that tells a named person in time.

**Live prototype:** https://vishwashah29.github.io/tea-post-contract-radar/

- Start with a guided walkthrough, or skip and explore on your own.
- The "About this prototype" button (bottom right) explains the client, stakeholders, scope, decisions and success metrics.
- Best on a laptop screen.

Tea Post, its people, suppliers, contracts and figures are invented. The look follows Sirion's public product screens and is not official. This is not affiliated with Sirion.

The whole prototype is one self-contained file, `index.html`. It loads one font from Google Fonts.
